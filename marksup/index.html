What's inside

Exam-first. Every feature points at the SSLC paper.

1

A journey across Tamil Nadu

The eight chapters are eight stops on a winding path across the state. Students always see where they are and what's next.

2

"See it" cards

Interactive visuals for the ideas students find hardest — solids in mensuration, Thales' theorem, slope, the roots of a quadratic — to move and explore, not just read.

3

Board-style practice

Textbook exercises plus original 2-mark and 5-mark questions written in the board's style, so practice looks like the exam.

4

Full mock papers

Mocks built from the textbook, previous-year questions and original questions, timed and weighted like the real SSLC paper.

5

AI tutor

Stuck on a step? Ask, and get that exact step explained in simple English in the context of the question.

6

Works on phone and web

Android app first, with a web version alongside for school computers and laptops, on the same account.

Syllabus

The complete Samacheer Kalvi Class 10 Maths textbook.

  6. Trigonometry
  7. Mensuration
  8. Statistics and Probability
