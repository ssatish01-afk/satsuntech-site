How we teach

Every topic goes through the same four steps.

1

See it

A short narrated animation — graphs, shapes, simulations — so the idea is pictured before it is calculated.

2

Learn it

A teacher voice explains while every line of working appears on screen. Pause, replay, ask "explain this step".

3

Practise it

Textbook problems, chapter tests and full papers. Mistakes are collected in a Mistake Book to revisit.

4

Parents see it

A short weekly report on what was studied, what went well and what to focus on next.
