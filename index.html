About SatSun Tech

A technology parent company with a growing digital product portfolio.

SatSun Tech is a digital technology company based in Chennai, India. Under the legal and proprietorship name Satsun, we operate as the parent company for multiple digital products built to simplify everyday experiences.

Our portfolio includes TalentUp, a Class 10 Mathematics learning app for students and parents, and solutions for child wellness, personal health and pet care.
