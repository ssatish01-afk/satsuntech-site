Features

Everything a Class 10 student needs for Maths, in one app.

Visual Learn

Start every topic here. Short narrated animations with graphs, shapes and simulations make each idea easy to picture and remember.

Step-by-step lessons

A teacher voice explains the topic while every calculation, diagram and graph develops on screen. Pause, replay and go at your own pace.

From the Book

Textbook-style problems solved one step at a time, with no skipped working, so you see exactly how each line leads to the next.

Ask AI

Stuck on a step? Tap "Explain this step" and the AI tutor explains that exact step in simple language.

Tests and Final Exam Preparation

Practise with chapter tests and full papers, then upload photos of your answer sheets for AI marking and feedback (Beta).

Mind Maps and Mistake Book

Revise each chapter as a mind map, and return to the questions you got wrong with their solutions.

For Parents

A clear, encouraging weekly report on your child's Maths.

TalentUp prepares a short weekly report on what your child studied, what went well and the next steps, with one simple way you can help at home. Share it by email or WhatsApp in one tap.

Parents can also choose to receive a weekly progress update on WhatsApp from TalentUp. It is off until a parent turns it on in the app, and you can stop it at any time by replying STOP or switching it off in Profile.
