What every TalentUp app has

The same toolkit, from Class 10 to Class 12.

Visual Learn

Narrated animations with graphs, shapes and simulations. Every topic starts here, so the idea is pictured before it is calculated.

Step-by-step lessons

A teacher voice explains while each calculation, diagram and graph develops on screen. Pause, replay, change the pace.

From the Book

Textbook problems solved one line at a time with no skipped working, so students see exactly how each step leads to the next.

Ask AI

Stuck on a step? Tap "Explain this step" and the AI tutor explains that exact step in simple language, in the context of the lesson.

Tests and AI marking (Beta)

Chapter tests and full papers. Write on paper, photograph the answer sheet, and get marks and feedback against the marking scheme.

Weekly parent report

What was studied, what went well, what to focus on next, and one simple way to help at home. Shareable by WhatsApp or email.

Pricing principle

Try everything first. Pay only when it's useful.

Every main feature can be used free on 3 days, with no payment details asked. After that, each app has a simple monthly Premium plan billed through Google Play or the App Store, which you can cancel any time.

How the free days work Refund & cancellation policy

Who holds the account? A parent or guardian signs in with Google, Apple or a mobile number and adds the student's name. The student learns; the parent sees the report. Accounts and all learning data can be deleted from inside the app at any time.
