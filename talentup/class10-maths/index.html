How it works

See it. Learn it. Practise it. Improve.

Every chapter follows the same loop, so students always know what to do next.

Visual Learn makes the idea click

A short narrated animation shows the concept — a graph moving, a shape unfolding, a simulation running.

Step-by-step lessons with a teacher

The teacher voice explains while each line of working appears on screen. Pause, replay, ask "explain this step".

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Does TalentUp follow the CBSE syllabus?

Yes. The app follows the Class 10 NCERT Mathematics textbook — all 14 chapters — and the board exam pattern. TalentUp is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CBSE or NCERT.

Who creates the account — the parent or the student?

The parent or guardian. You sign in with Google, Apple or a mobile number and add your child's name. Your child then uses the app to learn, and you receive the weekly report.

What does it cost?

Every main feature can be used free on 3 days, with no payment details asked. After that, Premium is a monthly plan billed through Google Play or the App Store; the current price is shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel any time from your store account.

Does it work on a laptop or desktop?

Yes. The web version at class10math.satsuntech.in works in any modern browser with the same account, and progress stays in sync with the phone app.

How does AI marking work?

After a test or full paper, you photograph the handwritten answer sheets in the app. The AI compares them against the marking scheme and returns marks and feedback for each question. It is in Beta: AI marks can differ from a teacher's, so please check important results with a teacher. The page images are not kept on our servers after marking.

What is in the weekly parent report?

What your child studied that week, where they did well, which topics need attention next, and one simple way to help at home. You can share it by WhatsApp or email. Automatic weekly WhatsApp updates are optional and off until you switch them on.

Is my child's data safe?

The app has no ads and no advertising SDKs. We don't sell personal information and we don't track children for advertising. You can delete the account and all learning data from Profile → Delete account at any time. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can we use one account on more than one device?

Yes. Progress syncs across Android, iPhone and the web. The app shows a notice when the account is signed in on another device.
