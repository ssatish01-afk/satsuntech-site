Common fixes

  • Subscription not recognised? Sign in with the same Google/Apple account or mobile number you purchased with. If it still doesn't show, email us the order ID from your Google Play or App Store receipt.
  • Teacher voice not playing? Check the device isn't on silent and that the app has an internet connection.
  • Signed in on another device? The app shows a notice; sign out there or continue on this device.

Cancel a subscription

  • Google Play: Play Store app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TalentUp → Cancel. Access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • App Store: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TalentUp → Cancel.

Refund requests for store purchases go through Google Play or Apple; see our refund & cancellation policy.

Delete your account

Open Profile → Delete account in the app, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.
