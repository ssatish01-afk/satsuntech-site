Refund & cancellation policy

Applies to all SatSun Tech appsLast updated: 6 November 2026

Try before you pay. Our learning apps let you use the main features free on a limited number of days with no payment details asked, so you can decide whether to subscribe. Nothing is charged until you choose a plan.

1. Where you bought it decides who refunds

Subscriptions and passes are sold through three channels. Each has its own process:

Bought throughHow to cancelHow to request a refund
Google Play (Android) Play Store app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → the app → Cancel Through Google Play's refund process (play.google.com → Order history). Google's refund policy applies; we cannot refund Play purchases ourselves.
App Store (iPhone) iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → the app → Cancel Through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's refund policy applies; we cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves.
Our website (Razorpay) Email support@satsuntech.com from the account email, or use the option in the app, where available Email us (section 3). Refunds go back to the original payment method.

2. Cancelling a monthly plan

  • Cancel at any time. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for; it simply does not renew.
  • Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store as above.
  • We do not give partial refunds for the unused part of a month, except as described in section 3.

3. Refunds for website (Razorpay) payments

  • Charged but no access: if you paid and the plan or pass was not activated on your account, email us within 7 days with the Razorpay payment ID and we will either activate it or refund it in full.
  • Duplicate payment: refunded in full.
  • Change of mind: for a monthly plan, email within 48 hours of payment and before using paid features, and we will refund in full. For a one-time pass (such as the MarksUp Exam Pass), email within 7 days of payment and before using paid features for a full refund. After that, passes are non-refundable.
  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval; your bank may take a few more days to show it.

4. One-time passes

A pass (for example, the MarksUp Exam Pass valid till 30 April 2027) is a single payment for access until the stated end date. It does not renew and no action is needed to stop it.

5. Price changes

If we change the price of a monthly plan, the new price applies from your next renewal, and the store will notify you in advance as its rules require.

6. Contact

Email support@satsuntech.com with the app name, the account email or mobile number, the payment date, and the order or payment ID. We reply within two working days.
