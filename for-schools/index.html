For schools

Visual, step-by-step maths for your Class 10 students.

TalentUp Class 10 Maths follows the NCERT textbook chapter by chapter, works on phones and on school computers, and sends parents a short weekly report. We're based in Chennai and happy to come and show it to your maths department.

Demo for teachers

A 30-minute session, in person in Chennai or online, walking the maths department through Visual Learn, lessons, tests and the parent report.

Students on any device

Android, iPhone and a web version for the school computer lab — one account, progress in sync.

Parents kept in the loop

Each parent receives a weekly report on what was studied and what to focus on next, so school and home pull in the same direction.

What teachers get

A teaching aid, not a replacement.

1

Visual Learn in class

Short animations for every topic that work on a projector — a quick way to open a lesson or revise before a test.

2

NCERT, chapter by chapter

All 14 chapters, in textbook order, with From the Book problems worked line by line.

3

Tests that mark themselves

Students write on paper, photograph the sheet and get marks against the scheme (Beta) — useful for extra practice between school tests.

4

Safe for students

No ads, no data selling, parent-held accounts, and deletion from inside the app. Full details in the privacy policy.
